# English Zone OS

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Planning

Owner: English Zone Academy

---

# Project Overview

English Zone OS is a complete Education Operating System designed exclusively for English Zone Academy.

The goal of this project is to build one unified platform that manages every aspect of the academy.

This is NOT just an LMS.

It is the digital operating system of the academy.

The platform will eventually replace spreadsheets, manual processes, disconnected tools and repetitive administrative work.

---

# Vision

Build the best education platform in the Middle East focused on English language learning.

Deliver a premium experience for students, teachers and administrators.

Automate daily operations.

Prepare the academy for future AI and CRM integration.

---

Every student.
